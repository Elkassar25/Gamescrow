About The Project

This graduation project focuses on solving trust and security issues in the secondary gaming market. The platform acts as a mediator between buyers and sellers to reduce fraud risks such as fake payments and account recovery scams.


The project combines the security of escrow systems with the speed and simplicity of digital marketplaces, creating a safer trading environment specifically designed for gamers.
